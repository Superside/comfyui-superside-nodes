# ============================================================================
# ARCHITECTURAL / INTERIOR STYLE GLOSSARY
# Source of truth for Superside Architectural Style Dial
# (modules/architectural_style_prompt_node.py)
#
# Purpose: a reusable vocabulary that DEFINES each interior style through its
# concrete elements - materials, cabinetry, furniture, lighting, ceilings,
# floors, fixtures and palette - broken down per room. Built from the reference
# LoRA training sets for three styles: TRANSITIONAL, TRADITIONAL, MODERN.
#
# TWO LAYERS (read this before editing):
#
#   LAYER A - GENERAL PROMPT DESCRIPTORS  (what the NODE actually emits)
#     Material / fabric / surface / metal / palette / light CATEGORIES that
#     describe the *character* of a style, plus a minimal room scene-anchor.
#     This is what goes into an inference prompt. It is deliberately general:
#     the LoRA already learned the look, so we steer it by material/texture/
#     palette, not by an object inventory.
#
#   LAYER B - DETAILED REFERENCE  (the STYLE > <ROOM> lists further down)
#     Exhaustive per-room element catalogs. Use these for TRAINING CAPTIONS or
#     human lookup - do NOT dump a whole list into one inference prompt: forcing
#     8-10 specific objects into a single frame causes clutter, duplicated /
#     melted objects and malformations. They stay here as reference only.
#
# All three styles share the same base look: warm evening real-estate
# photography, interior lights on, dark windows, magazine-quality staging.
#
# To edit: change Layer A here AND mirror it into _STYLE_CORE / _ROOM_ANCHOR in
# architectural_style_prompt_node.py so the two stay in sync. Layer B is
# reference and does not need mirroring.
# ============================================================================


# ############################################################################
# LAYER A - GENERAL PROMPT DESCRIPTORS  (mirrors the node; safe for prompts)
# ############################################################################

STYLE CHARACTER (material / fabric / surface / metal / palette / light)
  TRANSITIONAL : a warm balanced blend of classic and contemporary; oak and
                 soft painted wood; linen and wool textiles; quartz and marble
                 surfaces; subway tile; brushed brass and bronze; warm evening
                 light; greige/cream/taupe palette with muted navy and sage;
                 coffered ceilings and light millwork.
  TRADITIONAL  : a warm formal heritage feel; rich stained cherry/walnut/
                 mahogany wood; brick and stone; tufted leather, velvet, damask
                 and toile textiles; persian rugs; marble and butcher-block;
                 aged brass and bronze; warm incandescent light; warm brown/
                 cream/deep-green/navy/burgundy palette; wainscoting/beadboard.
  MODERN       : clean, minimal, contemporary; light oak/walnut/charcoal wood;
                 quartz, honed stone and concrete; matte black metal; flat-weave
                 wool, boucle and jute textiles; large black-framed glass;
                 balanced natural light; white/warm-oak/charcoal palette with
                 sage accents; uncluttered surfaces.

ROOM SCENE ANCHORS (minimal, style-agnostic - only what defines the room)
  living_room : a living room with a sofa, coffee table and fireplace
  bedroom     : a bedroom with a bed, nightstands and a dresser
  kitchen     : a kitchen with an island, cabinetry and pendant lighting
  dining_room : a dining room with a dining table and chairs
  bathroom    : a bathroom with a vanity, mirror and shower
  hallway     : a hallway with a console table and a runner rug

# LAYER B - DETAILED REFERENCE  (training captions / lookup - NOT for one prompt)
# ############################################################################
#
# Format:
#   STYLE > CORE          - the always-applied signature of the style
#   STYLE > <ROOM>        - elements specific to that room in that style

# ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
# STYLE 1 - TRANSITIONAL
# A balanced blend of classic and contemporary. Cleaner lines than traditional,
# warmer and more layered than modern. The "safe aspirational" real-estate look.
# ----------------------------------------------------------------------------

TRANSITIONAL > CORE
  - Blend of classic and contemporary; softened, balanced, uncluttered-but-warm
  - Walls: warm greige, soft white, taupe
  - Ceilings: crown molding, coffered ceilings, some tray ceilings
  - Millwork: wainscoting, board-and-batten, picture-frame paneling
  - Floors: medium white-oak / hardwood, muted patterned area rugs layered
  - Upholstery: oatmeal / linen, track-arm and rolled-arm sofas, upholstered beds
  - Case goods: mix of painted and medium-dark wood
  - Lighting: warm evening ambiance, glass globe pendants, lantern pendants,
    drum-shade chandeliers, white-shade table lamps, brass / bronze accents
  - Metals: brushed brass, oil-rubbed bronze, brushed nickel
  - Palette: greige, cream, taupe, warm white + muted navy and sage accents

TRANSITIONAL > LIVING_ROOM
  - Coffered ceiling, built-in shelving flanking a fireplace
  - Neutral sectional or sofa + accent chairs, wood coffee table
  - Layered area rug, globe or candelabra chandelier, white-shade table lamps
  - Framed landscape art, potted greenery

TRANSITIONAL > BEDROOM
  - Upholstered / tufted headboard, layered neutral bedding
  - Matching nightstands and table lamps, bench at the foot of the bed
  - Drum or lantern pendant, patterned area rug, soft lamplight

TRANSITIONAL > KITCHEN
  - Shaker cabinets in white, grey or navy
  - Quartz or marble waterfall island, subway-tile backsplash
  - Glass-and-metal pendant lights over the island
  - Stainless or paneled appliances, upholstered counter stools

TRANSITIONAL > DINING_ROOM
  - Rectangular wood dining table, upholstered parsons or spindle chairs
  - Linear or candelabra chandelier, sideboard / buffet
  - Wainscoting, framed art

TRANSITIONAL > BATHROOM
  - Quartz-top vanity, framed mirror, porcelain tile
  - Glass-enclosed shower, brushed-nickel or matte-black fixtures, sconces

TRANSITIONAL > HALLWAY
  - Console table with a lamp, patterned runner rug
  - Framed gallery wall, lantern or flush-mount pendant, wainscoting


# ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
# STYLE 2 - TRADITIONAL
# Classic, formal, heritage. Older-home character, rich woods, antiques,
# masonry, oil paintings, warm incandescent light.
# ----------------------------------------------------------------------------

TRADITIONAL > CORE
  - Classic, formal, symmetrical, heritage character
  - Fireplaces: brick and masonry with wood mantels
  - Wood: rich stained cherry, walnut, mahogany; carved detailing
  - Upholstery: tufted leather Chesterfields, skirted sofas, wingback chairs
  - Floors: herringbone / parquet and wide-plank; oriental / persian rugs
  - Art: framed oil landscape paintings in gilt frames
  - Millwork: wainscoting, beadboard, raised-panel doors, decorative crown molding
  - Lighting: warm incandescent, candelabra chandeliers, schoolhouse and lantern
    pendants, brass and ceramic table lamps, wall sconces
  - Metals: antique brass, aged bronze, polished nickel
  - Palette: warm brown, cream, deep green, navy, burgundy, gold

TRADITIONAL > LIVING_ROOM
  - Brick fireplace with a wood mantel, built-in bookshelves
  - Tufted leather Chesterfield or skirted sofa, wingback / club chairs
  - Carved wood coffee table, persian rug
  - Brass table lamps, candelabra chandelier, oil landscape paintings

TRADITIONAL > BEDROOM
  - Carved four-poster or spindle wood bed, damask and toile textiles
  - Oriental rug, brass and ceramic table lamps
  - Upholstered wingback chair, wood dresser, warm lamplight

TRADITIONAL > KITCHEN
  - Raised-panel stained-cherry or painted wood cabinets
  - Farmhouse sink, natural-stone or butcher-block counters
  - Handmade subway tile, bronze or brass pendants, dark vent hood
  - Warm wood floors

TRADITIONAL > DINING_ROOM
  - Formal wood dining table, upholstered or wood dining chairs
  - Shaded candelabra chandelier, wall paneling and wainscoting
  - Sideboard with lamps, wall sconces, framed landscape art, persian rug

TRADITIONAL > BATHROOM
  - Furniture-style wood vanity, marble counter
  - Classic subway or mosaic tile, polished-nickel or brass fixtures
  - Framed mirror, sconce lighting

TRADITIONAL > HALLWAY
  - Wood staircase with turned balusters, console table
  - Framed oil paintings, wall sconces
  - Schoolhouse or lantern pendant, runner rug, warm lamplight


# ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
# STYLE 3 - MODERN
# Clean, minimalist, contemporary. Slab cabinetry, waterfall stone, black metal,
# floor-to-ceiling glass, mid-century wood accents, uncluttered surfaces.
# ----------------------------------------------------------------------------

MODERN > CORE
  - Clean, minimalist, contemporary; uncluttered surfaces, negative space
  - Cabinetry: flat-panel / slab in light oak, walnut or charcoal
  - Stone: waterfall quartz and honed surfaces
  - Metals / hardware: matte black, blackened steel, minimal pulls
  - Windows: large black-framed, floor-to-ceiling glass, lots of daylight
  - Fireplace: linear gas fireplace with tile or stone surround
  - Furniture: low-profile upholstery, mid-century walnut accents, sculptural
  - Floors: wide-plank light oak, low-pile jute / wool rugs
  - Lighting: recessed, linear pendants, sculptural paper-lantern and globe
    fixtures, integrated LED, sleek track lighting
  - Art: large abstract pieces, minimal framing
  - Palette: white, warm oak, charcoal / black, greige + sage / olive accents

MODERN > LIVING_ROOM
  - Low-profile upholstered sofa in grey, blue or olive; walnut coffee table
  - Large black-framed windows, linear gas fireplace with tile / stone surround
  - Recessed and sculptural lighting, abstract art, floating media console
  - Minimal styling

MODERN > BEDROOM
  - Low platform bed, light-oak or walnut nightstands
  - Minimal neutral bedding with a sage or charcoal accent
  - Sculptural paper-lantern pendant or flush mount, large windows
  - Low-pile rug, a single framed artwork, clean walls

MODERN > KITCHEN
  - Flat-panel light-oak, charcoal or white slab cabinets
  - Waterfall quartz island, integrated or stainless appliances
  - Matte-black faucet and hardware, glass-globe or black pendants
  - Minimal open shelving, wide-plank oak floor

MODERN > DINING_ROOM
  - Round or rectangular oak / dark-wood table
  - Sculptural curved or cane dining chairs
  - Cluster or linear pendant, abstract art, minimal sideboard, large windows

MODERN > BATHROOM
  - Floating wood vanity, quartz top, backlit LED mirror
  - Large-format porcelain tile, matte-black fixtures, frameless glass shower

MODERN > HALLWAY
  - Minimal console table, black-framed gallery wall
  - Wall sconces or recessed lighting, clean walls
  - Oak floor, simple-railing staircase

  - Warm evening interior real-estate photography
